Composition invariant: wide 16:9, man and cabinet on right half, empty subdued wall/aisle on left 42-45% for existing title. Exactly one chair per cabinet receding in one coherent row, no background patrons. Hips visibly on seat and thigh support, natural wrist/fingers matching real handle grip. No added words, logos, watermarks, titles. Final output must convincingly resemble a real candid photo of an ordinary middle-aged customer rather than a polished generated illustration.
